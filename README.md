<h2>📋 What Is BidGuard?</h2>
<p>BidGuard Tender Comparison Hub is a powerful desktop application that helps you analyze procurement tender documents without an internet connection. It automatically checks for bid-rigging risks, finds contradictions in files, and creates verified evidence reports you can save locally. Perfect for procurement professionals, auditors, and compliance teams who need to review tender submissions thoroughly.</p>

<h2>✨ Key Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Offline-First Analysis</strong> – Works completely offline. Your data never leaves your computer.</li>
<li><strong>Bid-Rigging Detection</strong> – Flags suspicious patterns like identical pricing, similar formatting, or shared metadata between competing bids.</li>
<li><strong>Contradiction Finder</strong> – Compares multiple tender documents to spot inconsistencies in specifications, timelines, or pricing.</li>
<li><strong>Evidence Report Export</strong> – Generates professional PDF reports with timestamps and document references for audit trails.</li>
<li><strong>Local Storage</strong> – All your analyses and reports are saved directly on your computer for privacy and security.</li>
<li><strong>User-Friendly Interface</strong> – Simple drag-and-drop document loading with clear results displayed in an easy-to-read format.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📖 How to Use BidGuard</h2>
<h3>Step 1: Add Documents</h3>
<p>Click the "Add Files" button or drag tender documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX) directly into the main window. You can add multiple bids at once for comparison.</p>

<h3>Step 2: Run Analysis</h3>
<p>Click the "Analyze" button. BidGuard will scan all loaded documents for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Duplicate company names or addresses</li>
<li>Similar writing styles or formatting</li>
<li>Identical pricing structures</li>
<li>Matching metadata (author, creation date)</li>
<li>Contradictory specifications or timelines</li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 3: Review Results</h3>
<p>Results appear in a color-coded dashboard. Red flags indicate high-risk findings, yellow shows moderate concerns, and green means no issues detected. Click any flag to see details and source documents.</p>

<h3>Step 4: Export Reports</h3>
<p>Click "Export Report" to create a PDF summary of all findings. Reports include timestamps, document references, and detailed descriptions of each flag for compliance purposes.</p>

<h2>🛠️ Troubleshooting</h2>
<p><strong>Q: The application won't start after installation.</strong><br>
A: Make sure you have Windows 10 or later installed. Try running the application as administrator by right-clicking the icon and selecting "Run as administrator."</p>
<p><strong>Q: My documents aren't loading.</strong><br>
A: Ensure your files are in PDF, DOCX, or XLSX format. If using scanned PDFs, they must contain text (not just images).</p>
<p><strong>Q: I see a "No internet connection" message.</strong><br>
A: BidGuard works offline, but the first launch may check for updates. This message is normal and you can proceed by clicking "Continue Offline."</p>
